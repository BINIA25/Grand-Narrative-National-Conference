About the Conference

Ethiopia's contemporary political discourse is shaped by deep historical legacies, contested narratives of nationhood, and diverging visions for the future. This National Conference offers a rare opportunity to engage constructively with these unresolved questions and outline frameworks for institutionalizing a Grand Narrative that recognizes Ethiopia's diversity while establishing shared national purpose.
